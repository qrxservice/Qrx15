Bangla font: Kalpurush (Unicode)

Kalpurush.ttf is a free, open Unicode Bangla font (by Ekushey/OmicronLab)
designed to closely resemble the proprietary SutonnyMJ.

Why not SutonnyMJ directly?
SutonnyMJ is an ASCII/Bijoy font: it maps Bangla shapes onto Latin keyboard
codes. The app stores and displays Bangla as Unicode, which SutonnyMJ cannot
render (it would show garbage). Kalpurush gives the same SutonnyMJ look while
correctly rendering Unicode Bangla text.

Referenced by the @font-face in src/index.css, served at /fonts/Kalpurush.ttf.
